Suit alterations at mayLEHUONG: a focus on fit

A beautiful suit must fit the shoulders, waist, sleeves and trouser length. Complimentary suit alteration is one of mayLEHUONG's highlights, giving customers an added way to care for and adjust their clothing over time.

Items are checked in person according to the condition of the suit: sleeve length, waist adjustment, trouser hemming, or buttons and stitching. What can be altered depends on the construction, the amount of spare fabric and the wear of the garment.

Points to compare when choosing where to alter your suit

  • Free scope: ask clearly which items are covered, which products they apply to and whether any costs arise.
  • Fit after alteration: check the balance of sleeves, waist and length, not just the alteration price.
  • Pickup time: discuss when you need to wear it so a suitable completion time can be confirmed.

Each provider's policy may differ. When you contact mayLEHUONG, describe the condition of your suit and the alterations you need so we can confirm the complimentary scope before accepting the garment.
