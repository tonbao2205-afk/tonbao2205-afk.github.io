The problem

Fit compared: off-the-rack vs bespoke suits

Off-the-rack suits are cut to a standard size chart. These are the four most common problems at a try-on, and how bespoke tailoring fixes each one.

01

Shoulders and a gentle hourglass waist

Off-the-rack

  • Shoulders too wide, dropping past the shoulder bone and denting the top of the sleeve, or so tight they pinch the armpits.
  • A straight, boxy body, or an X-shaped pull across the front when buttoned.

mayLEHUONG bespoke

  • The shoulder seam stops right at the shoulder bone, with a smooth, rounded sleeve head.
  • A gently shaped hourglass waist that flatters a V shape and stays comfortable when you sit.
02

Sleeves the right length

Off-the-rack

  • Sleeves so long they cover the back of the hand and hide the shirt cuff, or so short the whole wrist shows when you bend your arm.

mayLEHUONG bespoke

  • The sleeve ends at the wrist bone, showing 1–1.5 cm of shirt cuff.
03

Trousers that hang clean, without a heavy break

Off-the-rack

  • Ready-made trousers come in one length, so they are often too long and bunch into several folds over the shoe.

mayLEHUONG bespoke

  • Trousers cut to your leg length and shoe opening fall straight and just touch the top of the shoe.
04

Your own build: sloping shoulders, back, hips

Off-the-rack

  • A gap at the back of the collar, horizontal folds below the neck and a back vent that flares open.

mayLEHUONG bespoke

  • A pattern cut from 25 body measurements: the collar hugs the shirt, the back lies flat, the vent hangs closed.

Privileges

5 handcrafted and custom details only a mayLEHUONG bespoke suit has

Details mass-produced suits cannot offer, and the reason a bespoke suit is worth more.

01

Hand pick-stitching on the lapel edge

Off-the-rack

  • Machine-sewn lapel and pocket-flap edges look flat and stiff, and the fused interlining can bubble.

mayLEHUONG bespoke

  • Our tailors hand-stitch tiny stitches along the lapel, collar, pocket flaps and sleeves, keeping edges soft and the shape lasting.
02

A hand-sewn Milanese lapel buttonhole

Off-the-rack

  • Lapel buttonholes are machine-punched with thin thread that frays, or are only fake.

mayLEHUONG bespoke

  • A Milanese buttonhole is hand-sewn stitch by stitch in silk around a raised cord, standing out crisply on the lapel.
03

Your name embroidered inside the chest

Off-the-rack

  • Only a mass-production label, the same for everyone who wears it.

mayLEHUONG bespoke

  • Your name or signature embroidered inside the chest, in the font and thread colour you choose.
04

Choose every detail yourself

Off-the-rack

  • Lapel style, buttons, lining colour and pockets are fixed; you take the design as it comes.

mayLEHUONG bespoke

  • Choose the lapel (peak, notch or shawl), silk lining colour, horn buttons, pockets, vents and working sleeve buttonholes.
05

Fittings before the suit is finished

Off-the-rack

  • You buy a finished jacket; if it does not fit, only patch-up alterations are possible.

mayLEHUONG bespoke

  • You try the jacket on while it is still basted in white thread, so we can adjust the waist, sleeve and trouser length before finishing.

The solution

How does made-to-measure solve this?

Taking 25 body measurements

The tailor measures shoulders, chest, waist, stomach, arms, legs and shoulder slope. Measurements are kept on each person's profile, so next time you don't start from scratch.

Fabric chosen for how you wear it

An everyday work suit needs a durable, wrinkle-resistant cloth. A wedding suit needs standout colour and sheen. A travel suit needs to hold its shape on the road. Our consultants choose the fabric to match that need.

Fitting and adjustments before delivery

You try the suit on before it is finished so the tailor can correct anything that is not yet right, instead of altering a suit that is already complete.

Lifetime seam warranty

mayLEHUONG offers a lifetime seam warranty on made-to-measure suits, so your suit lasts for years.

    Consultation and fabric choice

Conclusion

When should you choose made-to-measure?

  • You wear suits often and need a lasting fit.
  • Your build is hard to fit off the rack: broad shoulders, a fuller stomach, long arms or a slight frame.
  • You need a suit for an important occasion: a wedding, a launch, a partner meeting.
  • You want the right fabric for the way you'll wear it.

If any of these apply to you, made-to-measure is worth more than buying off the rack and altering. Leave your details and our tailors will contact you.
