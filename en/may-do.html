Materials of Distinction

Premium Fabric Collection

Discover premium fabrics carefully selected by mayLEHUONG from Italy and England, suited to every budget and bespoke need.

CERAZO Fabric

7,000,000 VND

Smart composition (Poly-Viscose Blend): A classic blend in the mid-range textile segment. Polyester fibers provide a sturdy frame with high tensile strength, excellent wrinkle resistance and crisp trouser creases throughout a 8-hour working day.
Feel on the skin: Viscose (Rayon) fibers derived from wood pulp relieve stuffiness. The fabric is soft, drapes naturally and absorbs perspiration fairly well, keeping the garment structured without any roughness.
Standard weight (300 g/m): Moderate thickness with a tight weave that resists sagging. The surface has an elegant matte finish, never shiny or cheap-looking.

GAZZARINI UOMO Fabric

Imported from Italy

9,000,000 VND

Tactile feel: The greatest difference in Italian fabric is its silky softness. A high content of wool gives a light, airy, breathable surface with no itch. A naturally luxurious sheen under the light.
Drape & silhouette: An ideal weaving weight keeps the suit sharp at difficult points such as the shoulders and lapels, while remaining light and soft with every movement of the body.
Classic palette: Deep, powerful shades: deep Navy, Charcoal, ash Grey and true Black. These are timeless colors that flatter the skin and are easy to coordinate.

DONATELLA Fabric

Imported from Italy

10,500,000 VND

Premium Micro Fiber technology: Ultra-fine fibers woven at very high density create a luxurious, silk-like sheen and an exceptionally soft touch against the skin.
Wrinkle resistance & lasting color: Its most valuable advantage is outstanding wrinkle resistance. Italian dyeing technology keeps distinctive tones (Yellow, Burgundy) vivid and fade-free through years of wear.
Ideal for: Perfect for formalwear (Tuxedo/wedding suits) that needs to shine under stage lights, or for gentlemen in the arts who want to express a bold aesthetic.

LOUIS Fabric

Imported from England

12,000,000 VND

Tightly woven structure: The hallmark of English fabric is its tightly twisted yarn, high-density weave and substantial weight. The suit takes shape superbly, lying smooth like armor that conceals any body imperfection.
Rock-solid durability: Excellent abrasion resistance, with little fraying or loss of shape after decades of wear. Superb wrinkle recovery when hung up.
A refined matte surface: Not flashy or glossy; its quiet, understated matte beauty conveys a luxury that only true connoisseurs of formalwear will recognize.

ROGER LA VIALE Fabric

Limited Edition

18,000,000 VND

A blend of heritage & technology: 70% Wool ensures a refined character: light and airy, with excellent temperature regulation (breathable) and a soft, natural drape that follows the body.
The "Travel Suit" secret: 30% high-strength technical Poly overcomes the tendency of pure wool to wrinkle. It forms a flawless wrinkle-resistant armor, with razor-sharp trouser creases even after a long flight or back-to-back meetings.
Melange effect: An international standard width of 58/60" and a refined weave create an eye-catching blended-color surface, especially distinguished in Grey and Burgundy.
