Purchase policies & terms

What you need to know about delivery, returns, warranty, payment, privacy and complaints when you shop or use our services at mayLEHUONG.

Updated: 08/11/2026

01

General terms of trade

The policies on this page apply to all purchases, bespoke orders, rentals and services on maylehuong.com and at mayLEHUONG showrooms.

  • Prices shown on the website are in Vietnamese dong. The final price of an order is the price our staff confirm with you before the transaction.
  • For bespoke garments, the price depends on the fabric collection and style you choose; it is quoted clearly before measuring and cutting.
  • An order is placed once mayLEHUONG confirms it with you by phone or Zalo.
  • Images marked "ảnh minh họa" (illustration) are for reference only; fabric colors on screen may differ from the real fabric.
02

Delivery policy

  • Free delivery on orders from 500,000₫.
  • Orders under 500,000₫: the delivery fee follows the carrier's rates and is given before shipping.
  • Nationwide delivery, usually 3–5 days depending on your address.
  • For bespoke garments, the collection date is set after measuring and choosing the fabric; you can collect at the showroom or have it delivered.
  • You may inspect the goods on delivery. If an item is the wrong style, wrong size or damaged, please call our hotline right away.
03

Returns & refunds

  • Ready-to-wear items can be returned or exchanged within 30 days of delivery.
  • Returned items must have all tags attached, be unworn, unwashed and unaltered, and come with the invoice.
  • Items that are the wrong style or size compared with the confirmed order, or have a manufacturing defect, are exchanged free of charge.
  • Bespoke garments are made to your own measurements: instead of a return, mayLEHUONG alters them free of charge until they fit the agreed measurements.
  • When a return is accepted and a refund is due, mayLEHUONG refunds you by the method you paid with; the refund time is given when we confirm your request.
04

Warranty & aftercare

  • Lifetime seam warranty on bespoke suits made at mayLEHUONG.
  • Free suit alterations for customers who had a suit tailored or bought one at mayLEHUONG; the scope of work is confirmed when we receive the garment.
  • Free steaming and pressing for customers who buy a suit at mayLEHUONG.
  • The warranty does not cover damage from improper use, burns, tears, chemicals or alterations made elsewhere.
05

Payment method

  • Cash on delivery (COD) for orders placed on the website.
  • Payment in person at our showrooms.
  • Bank transfer for ready-to-wear (in-stock) items. Please transfer only to the account details sent by mayLEHUONG staff when confirming your order via our official hotline or Zalo.
  • For bespoke garments, any deposit is stated when the order is finalized and deducted from the total when you collect.
  • mayLEHUONG confirms the amount paid and the balance due via Zalo or on the invoice.
06

Privacy policy

mayLEHUONG collects and processes personal data in accordance with Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP on personal data protection.

  • Information collected: name, phone number, email (if any), delivery address, body measurements (for bespoke garments) and what you send through our consultation forms.
  • Purpose: to confirm and fulfil orders, tailor to your measurements, deliver, provide warranty and customer care, and send order updates via Zalo.
  • Information is shared only with delivery companies or tailoring partners when needed to fulfil your order, or with competent authorities when required by law.
  • Information is kept on access-controlled systems for as long as needed to serve your order and warranty, and as required by law.
  • Your customer profile QR code or link lets you view your orders and measurements; please do not share it with others.
  • You may ask to view, correct or delete your personal information by contacting our hotline.
07

Complaints & dispute resolution

  • Send feedback or complaints via our hotline 0948.954.848, Zalo, email xuongvestcuoibh9999@gmail.com or in person at a showroom.
  • mayLEHUONG receives and reviews your complaint, then contacts you to agree on a resolution.
  • Both parties will first seek to resolve the matter through negotiation and mediation. If no agreement is reached, you may contact the state consumer protection authority or bring the matter before a court under Vietnamese law.
