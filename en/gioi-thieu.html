Milestones in Our History

From the early days in a traditional Western-suit tailoring village to a leading premium menswear brand.

history_edu
1960 – 1970

Origins of a Legacy

Our roots lie in a family tradition, with a grandfather who was a veteran master of Western tailoring. The workshop was proud to be part of the Hồ Gươm Premium Bespoke Cooperative (a central diplomatic facility) – where garments were crafted for heads of state of the era.

auto_fix
1985

Revival of a Traditional Craft Village

Seizing the opportunity as the economy opened up, the older generation became pioneers in laying the first bricks to revive the Từ Thuận Western-suit tailoring village (Hà Nội). The flame of passion and refined bespoke technique was formally passed down through the generations.

domain
2012

Laying a Solid Foundation

Founded Văn Hương tailoring workshop in Văn Từ, Hà Nội – the predecessor of Le Huong Fashion Co., Ltd., marking the shift from a family business to professional, larger-scale operation.

public
2013

Reaching the World

We expanded production and proudly exported premium garments bearing the mark of Vietnamese artisans to the markets of Qatar and many other demanding international markets.

verified
2018 – PRESENT

Expanding into the southern market with our showroom in TP. Biên Hòa (Đồng Nai). Inheriting more than half a century of heritage, mayLEHUONG we are now a premium uniform and suit partner to: Tín Nghĩa Corporation, Sonadezi, Tâm An Dental...
