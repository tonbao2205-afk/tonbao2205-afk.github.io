- Brand names stay unchanged: mayLEHUONG, Lê Hương, mayLEHUONG FASHION COMPANY LIMITED.
- Legal name "Công ty TNHH Thời Trang Lê Hương" → "レーフオン・ファッション有限会社 (Công ty TNHH Thời Trang Lê Hương)".
- These stay unchanged: hotline numbers, emails, URLs, prices, product codes, sizes (S, M, XL, 2XL…).
- Placeholders `{N1}` (product name), `{C1}` (product code), `{D1}` `{D2}`… (numbers):
  - Copy each one exactly, with the same count.
  - You may move them, but never drop or add one.
- Material Symbols icon names (`shopping_bag`, `arrow_forward`…) and pure code tokens: return them unchanged.
- Currency: keep `đ` / `₫` / `VNĐ` exactly as written after the number. Do not convert to 円.
- Punctuation inside Japanese sentences: 、。：！？「」（）.
- Many strings are fragments of a sentence split by inline markup:
  - Keep the separators ` · `, ` | `, ` – `, `→` as they are.
  - Keep any leading or trailing ":" / "·".
  - The fragment must read naturally when the pieces are joined (no spaces between pieces).
- ALL CAPS Vietnamese: just translate it, keeping Latin words in caps.
- Honorifics: Anh / Chị / Quý khách → お客様.
- No spaces between Japanese words.
