# Korean glossary (한국어) — mayLEHUONG website

Write natural, polite Korean marketing copy for a luxury tailoring house (합니다/하세요 style, 존댓말). Translate from the Vietnamese. The English and Chinese are references for meaning only.

## Fixed rules
- Brand names stay unchanged: mayLEHUONG, Lê Hương, mayLEHUONG FASHION COMPANY LIMITED.
- Legal name "Công ty TNHH Thời Trang Lê Hương" → "레흐엉 패션 유한회사 (Công ty TNHH Thời Trang Lê Hương)".
- Hotline numbers, emails, URLs, prices, product codes and sizes (S, M, XL, 2XL…) stay unchanged.
- Placeholders `{N1}` (product name), `{C1}` (product code), `{D1}` `{D2}`… (numbers):
  - Copy each one exactly, with the same count.
  - You may move them. Never drop or add one.
- Material Symbols icon names (`shopping_bag`, `arrow_forward`…) and pure code tokens: return them unchanged.
- Currency: keep `đ` / `₫` / `VNĐ` exactly as written after the number. Do not convert to 원.
- Many strings are fragments of a sentence split by inline markup:
  - Keep the separators ` · `, ` | `, ` – `, `→`, and any leading or trailing ":" / "·".
  - The fragment must still read naturally when the pieces are joined.
- ALL CAPS Vietnamese: just translate it (Korean has no case; keep Latin words in caps).
- Honorifics Anh / Chị / Quý khách → 고객님.
- Use normal Korean spacing.
